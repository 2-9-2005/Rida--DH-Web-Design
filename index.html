INTRODUCTION

Sir Allama Muhammad Iqbal was a visionary philosopher, poet, and politician born in Sialkot, Pakistan, who is widely regarded as the spiritual father of Pakistan. He pursued higher education in philosophy and law in Europe, studying at prestigious institutions like the University of Cambridge, Munich University, and Lincoln's Inn. Deeply influenced by Islamic history and Western philosophical thought, he dedicated his life to reviving the political and spiritual consciousness of Muslims in the Indian subcontinent. His profound philosophical insights and active role in the All-India Muslim League culminated in his historic 1930 Allahabad Address, where he famously presented the vision of an independent Muslim state. Today, he is revered globally as a monumental thinker and honored as the national poet of Pakistan.
